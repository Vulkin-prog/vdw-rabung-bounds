\begin{abstract}
The registered numerical result comprises nine direct Rabung bounds for
$W(3,k)$, $17\le k\le25$, headed by
$W(3,17)>31\,385\,622\,833$. Five of these are the strongest bounds obtained
for $17\le k\le21$ from an explicitly listed, dated corpus closed under the
Blankenship--Cummings--Taranchuk and Xu recurrences; the other four improve the
direct Rabung values for $22\le k\le25$ but not the best general bounds in that
corpus. Four archived two-colour primes due to Monroe also yield, in
particular,
$W(3,25)>628\,673\,328\,287$ and
$W(3,28)>2\,159\,051\,058\,266$. The GPU campaign only discovered candidate
triples. For each triple $(p,r,k)$, a stand-alone, separate-source CPU verifier
reconstructs the finite hypotheses of Rabung's theorem. A fail-closed audit of
thirteen manifests binds the code, commands and raw outputs and accepts all
$67$ registered local executions. All qualification runs and rechecks were
performed on one author-controlled PC; no independently administered external
replication has been performed. A secondary, model-conditional holdout
comparison is reported separately in an appendix.
\end{abstract}
