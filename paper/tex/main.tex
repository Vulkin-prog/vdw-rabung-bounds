\begin{abstract}
The registered numerical result comprises nine direct Rabung bounds for
$W(3,k)$, $17\le k\le25$, headed by
$W(3,17)>31\,385\,622\,833$. Conditional on the final fail-closed verification
manifests, five of these are the strongest bounds obtained for
$17\le k\le21$ from an explicitly listed, dated corpus closed under the
Blankenship--Cummings--Taranchuk and Xu recurrences; the other four improve the
direct Rabung values for $22\le k\le25$ but not the best general bounds in that
corpus. Four archived two-colour primes due to Monroe also yield, in
particular,
$W(3,25)>628\,673\,328\,287$ and
$W(3,28)>2\,159\,051\,058\,266$. The GPU campaign only discovered candidate
triples. For each triple $(p,r,k)$, a stand-alone, separate-source CPU verifier
reconstructs the finite hypotheses of Rabung's theorem; a fail-closed release
artifact binds its code, commands and raw outputs. A secondary,
model-conditional holdout comparison is reported separately in an appendix.
\end{abstract}
