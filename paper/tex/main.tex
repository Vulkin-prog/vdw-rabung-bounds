\begin{abstract}
The registered numerical result comprises nine direct Rabung bounds for
$W(3,k)$, $17\le k\le25$, headed by
$W(3,17)>31\,385\,622\,833$. Five direct bounds win for
$17\le k\le21$ in an explicitly listed source corpus audited through
29~August~2026. A primary-source review adds Berlekamp's 1968 bound
$W(3,24)>1\,082\,646\,556\,499$, which also wins at $k=25$ by length
monotonicity. Blankenship--Cummings--Taranchuk consequences win at
$k=22,23,26,27,28$, including
$W(3,28)>2\,159\,051\,058\,266$. A stronger textbook statement for $k=24$
is documented but excluded because no proof or verifiable derivation was
located. The GPU campaign only discovered candidate triples. For each triple
$(p,r,k)$, a stand-alone, separate-source CPU verifier reconstructs the finite
hypotheses of Rabung's theorem. A fail-closed audit of thirteen manifests binds
the code, commands and raw outputs and accepts all $67$ registered local
executions. All qualification runs and rechecks were performed on one
author-controlled PC; no independently administered external replication has
been performed. A secondary, model-conditional holdout comparison is reported
separately in an appendix.
\end{abstract}
