\begin{abstract}
We present three primitive Rabung certificates at three colours, giving nine
direct lower bounds for van der Waerden numbers $W(3,k)$ with $17\le k\le25$.
The five bounds for $17\le k\le21$ improve the earlier comparators identified
in a source audit updated on 26~September~2026, including the relevant
specializations of a stronger published statement whose proof status remains
unresolved in that audit. In particular, we obtain
$W(3,17)>31\,385\,622\,833$, improving the identified predecessor by a factor
of $2.02$. The GPU search supplies candidate triples $(p,r,k)$; a stand-alone
CPU verifier reconstructs the finite hypotheses of Rabung's theorem.
We prove the equivalence of the implemented boundary test with the published
criterion and provide a provenance-bearing finite closure under known
recurrences. Four earlier two-colour certificates from Monroe's distributed
project are re-verified and credited to that project. An empirical comparison
of search intensities is reported separately and is not used to establish
the lower bounds.
\end{abstract}
