\section{Data, code, and release status}\label{s:data}

The publication-oriented repository is
\href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/vdw-rabung-bounds}{\texttt{Vulkin-prog/vdw-rabung-bounds}}.
It was exported under an explicit allowlist from the development repository at
the immutable source commit
\texttt{02796ae9e08da5b051a94b2d9001763908375f6e}.  At the time of this
revision it remains a private preparation repository, not a citable release.
Its machine-readable status ledger leaves, among others, the claim,
campaign-recovery, ordered-prime, CUDA, external-replication, rights, and DOI
gates open.  The
step-by-step operator and archival procedures are maintained in
\texttt{REPRODUCIBILITY.md}; here we distinguish the three levels of evidence
needed to interpret the paper.

\paragraph{Individual triples.}
The registry \texttt{audit/claims.json} contains exactly thirteen succinct
Rabung certificates $(p,r,k)$.  A reader can check one triple without
reproducing the campaign by rebuilding the stand-alone verifier, which re-establishes
primality, the congruence and size preconditions, and conditions
$(a)\wedge(B^*)$.  Release-level evidence additionally binds each verdict to
the exact source, binary, command, environment, and raw output in a fail-closed
manifest.  Those final manifests have not yet been populated: the placeholder
under \texttt{results/claims/} is not evidence.  Four fixed triples must also be
rechecked on hardware administered by four distinct external operators.

\paragraph{Derived tables and the holdout analysis.}
The separate registry \texttt{audit/baselines.json} records published,
archived, and current strict lower bounds with source locators.
\path{tools/close_baselines.py} closes those inputs under the registered
recurrences, preserves a provenance path for each output, and generates the
tables used in Section~\ref{s:bounds}. The survivor list, reconstructed
holdout ledger, and generated audit reproduce the disjoint counts and model
means reported in Sections~\ref{s:prereg} and~\ref{s:density}, conditional on
the retained event list.  These artifacts reproduce the reported derivations;
they do not by themselves re-evaluate every prime or establish complete
campaign coverage.

\paragraph{The complete campaign audit.}
Claims about exhaustive coverage of
$[9.7\times10^8,2\times10^9)$ require the full $515$-chunk record.  Material
that was historically ignored must first be recovered read-only from the
campaign PC, including checkpoint evidence for chunk~7, and mapped without
inventing unavailable metadata.  The release must then establish byte-level
identity between the scanner and separate CPU ordered-prime streams on every
chunk; agreement of aggregate congruence counts is not a substitute.  The four
historical scanner blobs and their revision map, together with the preserved
aggregate re-scan of the first $17$ chunks, are already registered, but neither
that re-scan nor the current summaries close the full campaign gate.  A complete
GPU campaign rerun is required only if recovery reveals a material gap that
cannot otherwise be closed.

The cyclic $W_c$ pilot and its proof artifacts are outside the scientific
scope of this records paper and are intentionally absent from this repository.
The archived two-colour phase-2 source is credited and located, but a verbatim
third-party web capture is not redistributed unless its rights status is
resolved.  Outbound licences for code, paper, and data also remain an explicit
author decision.  These rights and provenance records support redistribution
and priority attribution; they do not convert an archival coverage statement
into a newly audited fact.

After the scientific, campaign, replication, and rights gates pass, the final
metadata and DOI will be inserted into the exact release candidate.  A checked,
immutable tagged archive---not a moving branch---will then be deposited and
cited.  Until that archive and its external receipt exist, this private
repository and manuscript are not represented as a complete citable release.
