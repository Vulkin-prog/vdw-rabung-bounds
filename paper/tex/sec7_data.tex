\section{Data, code, and release status}\label{s:data}

The publication-oriented repository is
\href{https://github.com/Vulkin-prog/vdw-rabung-bounds}{\texttt{Vulkin-prog/vdw-rabung-bounds}}.
It was exported under an explicit allowlist from the development repository at
the immutable source commit
\texttt{02796ae9\allowbreak{}e08da5b0\allowbreak{}51a94b2d\allowbreak{}
90017639\allowbreak{}08375f6e}.  At the time of this
revision it remains a private preparation repository, not a citable release.
Its machine-readable status ledger records the local claim, campaign-recovery,
ordered-prime, and CUDA evidence as passed, while clean final CPU/document
replays, rights, and DOI/tag freeze remain open.  The absence of external
replication is disclosed but is not a publication gate.  The
step-by-step operator and archival procedures are maintained in
\texttt{REPRODUCIBILITY.md}; here we distinguish the three levels of evidence
needed to interpret the paper.

\paragraph{Individual triples.}
The registry \texttt{audit/claims.json} contains exactly thirteen succinct
Rabung certificates $(p,r,k)$.  A reader can check one triple without
reproducing the campaign by rebuilding the stand-alone verifier, which re-establishes
primality, the congruence and size preconditions, and conditions
$(a)\wedge(B^*)$.  The current exact set of thirteen fail-closed manifests
binds every verdict to the source, binary, command, environment, and raw
output.  Its audit accepts all $67$ required executions, including the two
Montgomery-free high-range witnesses.  This local evidence combines GPU checks
with separate-source CPU rechecks; none is an independently administered
replication.  External rechecks remain an optional avenue for corroboration.

\paragraph{Derived tables and the holdout analysis.}
The separate registry \texttt{audit/baselines.json} records admitted published,
archived, and current strict lower bounds with source locators, while
\texttt{publication/bibliography-audit.json} records the search methods,
version pins, priority boundaries, and the published statement excluded for
lack of a verifiable proof. \path{tools/close_baselines.py} closes the admitted
inputs under length monotonicity and the registered recurrences, preserves a
provenance path for each output, and generates the tables used in
Section~\ref{s:bounds}. The survivor list, reconstructed
holdout ledger, and generated audit reproduce the disjoint counts and model
means reported in Appendices~\ref{s:prereg} and~\ref{s:density}, conditional on
the retained event list.  These artifacts reproduce the reported derivations;
they do not by themselves re-evaluate every prime or establish complete
campaign coverage.

\paragraph{The complete campaign audit.}
Claims about exhaustive coverage of
$[9.7\times10^8,2\times10^9)$ use the authenticated $515$-chunk record.  Its
checkpoint covers every chunk; $514$ rows also have an exact journal match,
while chunk~7 is explicitly and only recovered from the checkpoint.  The
archive contains $498$ historical per-chunk histograms for chunks~18--515.  The
earlier interval has no manufactured per-chunk cells: its preserved evidence is
one aggregate re-scan of chunks~1--17.  A separate audit compared the scanner
and CPU ordered-prime streams byte for byte on all $515$ chunks and found the
same $48\,823\,489$ primes.  Thus the aggregate first-17 re-scan closes its
stated count role, while ordered-prime identity supplies the stronger
enumeration check; no complete GPU campaign rerun is required.

The cyclic $W_c$ pilot and its proof artifacts are outside the scientific
scope of this computational-evidence paper and are intentionally absent from this repository.
The archived two-colour phase-2 source is credited and located, but a verbatim
third-party web capture is not redistributed unless its rights status is
resolved.  Outbound licences for code, paper, and data also remain an explicit
author decision.  These rights and provenance records support redistribution
and priority attribution; they do not convert an archival coverage statement
into a newly audited fact.

After the scientific, campaign, and rights gates pass, the final metadata and
DOI will be inserted into the exact release candidate.  A checked,
immutable tagged archive---not a moving branch---will then be deposited and
cited.  Until that archive and its post-upload byte-identity deposit receipt
exist, this private repository and manuscript are not represented as a complete
citable release.
